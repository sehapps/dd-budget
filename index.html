Where does your data live?

Short answer: with you.

  • Your financial data stays on your device. No income, expense or balance figure is sent to our servers.
  • The app works offline. Add transactions and read your charts with no connection.
  • Cloud backup is entirely optional. It is off by default, and you can use the app to the fullest without ever turning it on. If you'd rather nothing left your device, you can back up to a file instead.
  • Even when it's on, the backup is yours. It uploads to your own Google Drive account, not our servers, and access stays with you.
  • Your data comes with you. Export to Excel any time and take it to whatever else you want to use.
